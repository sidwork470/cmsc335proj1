Squirrels

Squirrels are some of the cutest furriest animals in the wild. They are small, brown mammals with a huge tail. Squirrels are often seen in neighborhood backyards or woods and forests always nibbling on small nuts and acorns.

California Ground Squirrel West Coast of the United States and Baja California burrow undergrounds Some of these squirrels hibernate through the winter, which is dependent on the location's weather conditions. These squireels do not live alone, they live with close buddies

Humboldt's Flying Squirrel Confierious and Mixed Confierious Forests of North America These squirrels love plants, bird eggs, fungi and insects and sap.
